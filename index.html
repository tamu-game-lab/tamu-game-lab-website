About

The Game Lab

The Texas A&M Game Lab designs, builds, and studies games and extended reality (XR) experiences for health, rehabilitation, learning, and creative expression. Every project in the showcase is a working prototype, developed and evaluated with real users.

The Game Lab is intentionally small: projects are faculty-led and student-built, giving student researchers direct experience in research design, development, and evaluation. Several projects are collaborations with partner researchers at Texas A&M and beyond.
